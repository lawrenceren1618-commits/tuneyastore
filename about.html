Business overview

Shenzhen Colorful Youjia E-commerce Co., Ltd. is an e-commerce company founded in 2023. Through TuneYa Store, we sell products online and manage order fulfillment, including coordination with parcel carriers for last-mile delivery.

To improve customer experience and handle delivery disputes efficiently, we maintain integrations with carrier tracking systems and may retrieve proof-of-delivery information for shipments related to our business.

What this website is for

This site is the public company presence used for business identity, privacy disclosures, and authorized integrations with logistics providers such as UPS, FedEx, USPS, and multi-carrier tracking platforms.

本网站用途

本站为公司对外公开信息与合规披露页面，用于业务身份说明、隐私政策公示， 以及申请/使用 UPS、FedEx、USPS 及多承运商轨迹平台等授权接口。

Proof of delivery

Where carriers provide POD documents or signatures, we archive them for customer service and claims support.

在承运商提供 POD 文件或签收信息时，我们归档用于客服与理赔支持。

API data use statement

We request carrier and tracking API access solely to track shipments associated with Shenzhen Colorful Youjia E-commerce Co., Ltd. / TuneYa Store and our customers. We do not sell tracking data, do not scrape unrelated packages, and do not provide unrestricted public tracking of third-party shipments.

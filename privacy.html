2. Information we collect

  • Contact data: name, email, phone number when you contact support.
  • Order data: order identifiers and shipping details needed to fulfill purchases.
  • Shipment data: tracking numbers, carrier names, tracking events, delivery status, and proof-of-delivery records when provided by carriers.
  • Technical data: basic server logs (IP address, browser type, timestamps) for security and site reliability.

3. How we use information

  • Process and deliver orders.
  • Provide shipment status updates and customer support.
  • Retrieve and archive proof of delivery for dispute resolution and claims.
  • Maintain security, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.
  • Integrate with authorized logistics APIs (for example UPS, FedEx, USPS, or multi-carrier tracking providers) solely for shipments related to our business.

4. Legal bases

Depending on applicable law, we process data to perform a contract (order fulfillment), pursue legitimate interests (service quality, fraud prevention, dispute handling), and/or comply with legal requirements. Where consent is required, we will request it.

5. Sharing

We may share necessary data with:

  • Parcel carriers and tracking/API providers to obtain tracking and POD information.
  • Payment, hosting, and IT service providers acting on our instructions.
  • Authorities when required by law.

We do not sell personal information.

6. International transfers

Because carriers and cloud providers may process data outside China, shipment-related data may be transferred internationally under appropriate safeguards and each provider’s terms.

7. Retention

We retain order and shipment records (including POD where available) for as long as needed for customer service, accounting, dispute handling, and legal retention requirements, then delete or anonymize them when no longer necessary.

8. Security

We apply reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect credentials and customer data, including restricted access to API keys and encrypted transport (HTTPS).

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal data, and you may contact us about privacy questions at support@tuneyastore.com.

10. Children

Our services are directed to businesses and adult customers. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
